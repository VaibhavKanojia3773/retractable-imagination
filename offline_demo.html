A SMALL, INSPECTABLE SYSTEM

Retract evidence,
not consequences.

Static occupancy factors + learned sensor scores + risk-aware planning. The agent's five past actions remain fixed when the evidence is removed.

01

Encode a measurement

A logistic sensor model maps detector features and quality to an additive log-likelihood score.

02

Keep its identity

Every contribution retains a source, timestamp, spatial factor and revocation status.

03

Repair and replan

Subtract revoked contributions; invalidate dependent cached forecasts. Replan globally from the actual current position.

RETRACTABLE IMAGINATION / RESEARCH PROTOTYPE

Simulated scene. No robot, general recurrent-state unlearning, or universal physics guarantee. Route-survival values assume independent static occupancy.
